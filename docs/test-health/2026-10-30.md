## Bottleneck

`cargo test --workspace` is mostly the attach test binary. Its three backpressure tests
(slow/stalled terminal) are wall-clock bound: 32 + 30 + 20 s of fixed waits and floods, running
side by side, and they set the 38 s. Everything else in the workspace finishes in under 3 s per test.
Regress comes second: xfail tests that time out waiting for features vtmux lacks (10-19 s each), which
should shrink as features land.

Nothing needs upkeep at 69 s. If the gate nears budget, first shorten the backpressure tests' stall
windows, or move them behind a separate `--ignored` slow tier.
