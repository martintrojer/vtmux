The first release candidate of vtmux, the drop-in tmux 3.8 replacement. From here to 3.8, fixes only. From beta3, upgrade with no restart: `tmux vtmux-upgrade -b <new binary>`.

## New

- **Static Linux builds.** musl tarballs for x86_64 and aarch64 run on EL9, Debian 11 and Alpine: `mise use -g "github:martintrojer/vtmux[matching=musl]"`.
- **Faster under load.** 20,000 commands piped into `-C` take 118 ms (tmux 180 ms), and commands answer in 13 ms while a client floods keys (a huge paste).

## Fixed

- A live upgrade no longer loses or garbles what you type during it, even mid-paste, and never leaves your terminal raw.
- No more stray blinking cursor on Ghostty, and the terminal's mouse, cursor and modes now match tmux after every popup, copy mode, detach and upgrade.
- `capture-pane -J` matches tmux, so tmux-resurrect saves the same pane contents it does with tmux.

## Removed

- **Save and restore.** Use [tmux-resurrect](https://github.com/tmux-plugins/tmux-resurrect) and [tmux-continuum](https://github.com/tmux-plugins/tmux-continuum): both are tested against vtmux. Drop any `vtmux-persist*` lines from your config.

## Reviewed

A final panel of 9 reviewers from two model families found no critical or high defects; every medium was fixed before this release.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/martintrojer/vtmux/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

`mise use -g github:martintrojer/vtmux` (mise waits a day before it offers a new release), the tarballs below, or `cargo install --locked vtmux@3.8.0-rc.1` (needs Zig 0.17).
